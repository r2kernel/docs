# Security Policy

## Reporting A Vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities in R2Kernel privately by email to
contact@librerobotics.org. Do not report them in a public issue, pull request
or discussion, and do not disclose them publicly while they are being
addressed.

Include as much of the following as you can:

- The affected component and version: runtime, installer, SDK, Web Console or
  documentation, and the R2Kernel release or SDK version.
- The environment: operating system, architecture and any relevant
  configuration.
- Steps to reproduce, ideally a minimal public reproducer.
- The impact you observed or expect, such as what an attacker could read,
  change or disrupt.

Send only what is needed to describe the issue. Do not include credentials,
customer data or other personal data, and test only systems you own or are
authorized to test.

We acknowledge reports, investigate them and coordinate the fix and its public
disclosure with the reporter. Response and fix times depend on the report and
are not guaranteed.
