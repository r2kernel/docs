# Security Policy

## Reporting A Vulnerability

Report possible security vulnerabilities in R2Kernel privately through the
[Librerobotics contact form](https://www.librerobotics.org/#contact).
Do not post the report in a public issue, pull request, or discussion.
Do not share the details publicly while the issue is being addressed.

Include these details when possible:

- The affected component, such as the runtime, installer, SDK, Web Console, or
  documentation. Include the R2Kernel release or SDK version.
- Your operating system, architecture, and relevant settings.
- Steps to reproduce the issue. Include a small example if possible.
- What an attacker could read, change, or disrupt.

Share only what is needed to explain the issue. Do not include credentials
such as passwords or tokens. Do not include customer data or other personal
data. Only test systems you own or have permission to test.

We acknowledge and investigate reports. We work with the reporter on the fix
and its public disclosure. We cannot guarantee response or fix times.
